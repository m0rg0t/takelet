## Generation prompt

Create one finished landscape Open Graph social-sharing card for Takelet, a free open-source native macOS screen recording and product-demo editor. Requested canvas 1200 x 630 pixels, wide 1.91:1. A polished editorial software poster, very clean and confident with generous negative space; deep near-black forest green background #101411, off-white #f0f3ec typography, pale lime #d3f6a6 accents, subtle dark green #1a201b layering. Exact text only: small brand wordmark 'takelet' near top left with a small pale-lime rounded rectangle containing a dark right-pointing play triangle; large main headline on two lines 'One take.' then 'A clearer story.' with 'clearer' in elegant italic serif contrasting with the modern sans-serif other words. Below, a clearly legible supporting line 'Open source screen recorder & demo editor' and a small 'Made for macOS' caption. On the right side, a tasteful abstract composition of three overlapping rounded cinematic frames, a tiny play triangle, and a thin filmstrip/timeline motif, using understated lime edge highlights and soft restrained shadow. These are abstract graphic shapes, not a fake app screenshot. Keep all typography high contrast, perfectly spelled, well inside 64px safe margins; make the headline large enough for a social feed thumbnail. The atmosphere should match a refined native Mac creative app. No photos, no people, no external logos, no browser chrome, no website URL, no ratings, no price, no badges, no invented UI, no watermark. Deliver the finished branded card.
